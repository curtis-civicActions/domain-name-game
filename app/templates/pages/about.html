This is a simple domain-name matching game built with Flask and Tailwind CSS. The game challenges players to match domain names with their corresponding IP addresses.

To play, go to the start screen, choose the game mode with the toggle, and click Get Question. A question and several answer choices will appear as clickable cards. Pick the card you think is correct, and the app checks your answer through the page’s interactive JavaScript/HTMX flow. The overall goal is to practice recognizing which domains belong to which people and vice versa.

The app stores people and the domains they own in MongoDB, either by entering them manually through the data form or by loading them from a spreadsheet. Once there is data in the database, the game generates a question that asks you to match a person to a domain or match a domain to its owner.
